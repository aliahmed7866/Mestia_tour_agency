Good questions. Straight answers.

Does a booking request reserve my place?

No. Choose an activity and use Request booking to send your preferred date, group size and contact details. We check availability and prepare a quote. Your place is confirmed only after you accept the quote, any required deposit is checked, and we send final confirmation.

The request form does not take a payment.

Where can I find the price and what’s included?

Each listing shows its price when one is set, plus its inclusions and exclusions. “Price on request” means you’ll receive a quote for your dates and group. Don’t assume meals, entrance tickets, a guide or a room are included unless they are listed. Your final quote sets out the agreed services and total before you accept.

Can I request any date?

Use the season notes on the activity page to choose a suitable time. A season is a planning window, not a live availability calendar. High routes, roads and lifts need a current check, even in summer. For a longer trip, enter your preferred starting date.

What if the weather changes?

Mountain plans sometimes need to move. A different day, a lower route or a village visit may be possible, depending on conditions and availability. We’ll agree any change with you; the booking terms attached to your quote govern cancellation and refunds.

How do I choose a walk, a 4×4 trip or a family outing?

Start with the walking, driving and difficulty notes on the listing. A full hike and a 4×4 outing to the same destination can be very different days. A 4×4 trip can still involve rough roads and walking. Add children’s ages, relevant access needs or concerns about the route to your request. Suitability depends on the activity, your group and current conditions.

Can I fit a mountain trip around my flight?

Keep arrival day light and leave room for road delays. For an onward flight, we suggest staying near the departure airport the night before instead of relying on a tight connection from Mestia. Include your airport, flight date and time when requesting a transfer; timings must be agreed before booking.

Can I add a guesthouse stay or a transfer?

Yes—choose your main activity and put the extra nights or journeys in the optional notes. Browse the guesthouse and transfer listings for details first. These are separate arrangements unless your listing includes them, and each needs an availability check and an agreed price.

What happens after I send my request?

You’ll receive a request reference and a private status link. Save that page: it shows your request and, when prepared, your quote and booking updates. Keep the link private because it contains your trip details. There’s no need to send the same request again on WhatsApp.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

Use the message form on your private status page to request a change or cancellation. Your current arrangements stay in place until the host reviews and responds. Cancellation charges and any refund depend on the terms attached to your accepted quote; sending a message does not automatically cancel a booking.

I have a question about one particular activity. Where should I ask?

Use the WhatsApp option on that activity’s page to start with a message about the trip you’re looking at. Review or edit the draft before sending it in WhatsApp. Opening a link does not send a message.

If you already have a request, use your private status page or include your request reference so we can find the details.
