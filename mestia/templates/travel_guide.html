01 / Find your season

Pick the month for the trip.

July–September

The usual Upper Svaneti trekking window. High routes still need a fresh weather and trail check; a summer date alone does not settle the plan.

April–June

Start with Mestia, village visits and lower outings where conditions allow. Snow can remain on high passes in June. Keep the ambitious walk optional.

October–November

Allow for shorter days and new snow. Ask about current access before choosing a high route. A town or cultural visit may fit better.

Winter

Plan around Hatsvali or Tetnuldi when roads, snow and lifts permit. Confirm the resort and any instructor separately; summer walking itineraries do not carry over.

02 / Give it some room

A day out, or a few days away?

One full day

Choose one main outing: a village day in Ushguli, a Chalaadi viewpoint walk, or Koruldi. The full Koruldi hike and the rough-road 4×4 outing are different days with different demands.

Three days based in Mestia

Give Chalaadi, Ushguli and Koruldi separate days, changing their order if needed. Our planning suggestion is to arrive before day one and leave after day three: usually four nights. Rooms and transfers need their own arrangements.

The four-day village trek

The Mestia–Ushguli walk needs four walking days, plus arrival, the return journey and room for a weather change. Confirm overnight stops, luggage plans and current river conditions with the guide.

03 / Make the journey part of it

Keep arrival day light.

A transfer from Kutaisi or Zugdidi deserves space for the mountain road and stops. Save the major hike for another day. For an onward flight, our planning preference is an overnight near the departure airport instead of a tight same-day connection.

Let us know your arrival point and flight time before settling the itinerary.

04 / Before you choose

A few fair questions.

Can children join?

Tell us their ages, walking experience and comfort with long drives. Suitability depends on the route, conditions and your group; there is no blanket “all ages” promise. Ask about short walks and a flexible turnaround.

What happens if the weather changes?

Discuss a lower outing, a village visit, a different day or postponement. An alternative still needs an access check. Your agreed booking terms govern changes and cancellations.

Can we combine a stay, a tour and a ride?

Yes—send one enquiry with your dates and group size. We discuss the separate arrangements, availability and price before confirming anything.
