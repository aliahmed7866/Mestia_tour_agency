# Short trips and a simpler way to choose

Reviewed 5 October 2026. The existing catalogue already covers the main Svaneti day routes shown by TravelMestia and Budget Georgia. This review adds two useful formats, rather than repeating those routes under more names.

| Added draft | Proposed plan | Reason for the choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heshkili, a little above it all | A 2–3 hour return viewpoint outing from Mestia, with short optional walks. | A small outing fills the gap between a town visit and a full mountain day. GNTA places Heshkili near Mestia and identifies its Laila panorama. Swing use and refreshments depend on local arrangements. |
| Three days, one Mestia base | Chalaadi, Ushguli and Koruldi by 4×4 on separate days. Arrive before the programme and leave the day after. | Travellers can choose a coherent short break without combining three enquiries. The proposed stay normally needs four Mestia nights; rooms and regional transfers are arranged separately. |

The Heshkili duration and June–September core season are original planning allowances. The full three-day programme uses July–September because its highest outing is Koruldi. A month label never establishes current road or trail access. Day order can change after a local forecast and access check. The Chalaadi route ends at a safe viewpoint, away from the glacier ice, and does not run in rain or fog.

## What the reference sites informed

[TravelMestia](https://www.travelmestia.com/) puts the destination, departure base, duration and season near the first decision. Its [Heshkili page](https://www.travelmestia.com/heshkili-swing-tour) demonstrates a short viewpoint format. Its own season labels are inconsistent, so they have not been imported as operating guarantees.

[Budget Georgia's Mestia catalogue](https://www.budget-georgia.com/tour-destination/mestia/) separates trips by starting city and distinguishes day outings from longer tours. Its [Ushguli page](https://www.budget-georgia.com/tour/day-tour-to-ushguli/) keeps an enquiry separate from the itinerary and practical details. Its [two-day Kutaisi–Svaneti programme](https://www.budget-georgia.com/tour/2-days-tour-from-kutaisi-to-sventi/) illustrates demand for short packages; our version gives the mountain outings full days and places the long regional drives outside them. These are original proposed routes, not departures operated by either reference business.

The useful interface choices are a clear trip title, a few comparable facts, a short introduction and a direct enquiry action. The complete route, requirements and weather alternatives belong on the detail page, where the guest can open them when needed. Commercial terms, prices, staff claims, reviews, photographs and booking guarantees from other operators have not been imported.
