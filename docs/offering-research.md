# Georgia offering research and route decisions

Reviewed 5 October 2026. These are original proposed itineraries, prepared for a Mestia-based operator to review and price. They are installed as editable drafts. Research establishes a sensible starting plan; the owner still chooses the actual guide, vehicle, accommodation, group limit, terms and departure conditions.

## What informed the catalogue

[TravelMestia](https://www.travelmestia.com/) makes its local departure point, trip format and season easy to find. [Budget Georgia](https://www.budget-georgia.com/) separates trips by starting city and distinguishes the itinerary from inclusions and exclusions. Those are useful presentation choices. Their commercial terms, staff claims, prices, reviews and photography are not this business's assets or commitments.

Destination facts were checked against the Georgian National Tourism Administration (GNTA), Svaneti Destination Management Organization, Transcaucasian Trail (TCT), Agency of Protected Areas and Mountain Trails Agency. Every preset retains its own source URLs and research date in its private admin notes. Driving allowances, rest stops, overnight choices and turnaround plans are our conservative planning judgments, not official journey guarantees.

## Seasons: how the presets use them

These are planning windows, not live forecasts or promises that a road, trail or lift will be open. The catalogue's month filter helps guests narrow ideas; it does not create availability or confirm a booking.

| Period | Svaneti planning approach | Useful alternatives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| December–March | Winter resort logistics only after snow, lift and road checks. January–March is the preset's core window. No summer trekking schedule carried into winter. | Mestia culture visits where access permits; city-based arrangements elsewhere. |
| April–May | Keep high trails conditional. Snowmelt and remaining snow make a calendar-only decision unsuitable. | Kutaisi-area visits and Kakheti cultural touring, subject to each site's operating conditions. |
| June | Lower outings may work after inspection. Do not treat a clear road or green valley as evidence that a high pass is ready. | Village days and flexible Mestia stays; revise the route if necessary. |
| July–September | Core high-route window. Rain, storms and river conditions still determine each day's plan. | Keep a spare day and a lower-level alternative in longer trips. |
| October–November | Shorter daylight and new snow narrow options. October mountain outings need fresh local confirmation; November is not a default trekking month. | Cultural visits, lowland routes, or a postponed mountain trip. |

TCT identifies July–September as the usual Upper Svaneti hiking season, with snow possible on higher sections outside it. GNTA describes strong variation between humid western lowlands, the mountains and eastern Georgia; a single national weather calendar would be misleading. We deliberately avoid route-specific temperature or rainfall tables based on coarse regional averages. Sources: [TCT practical information](https://transcaucasiantrail.org/en/hike/upper-svaneti-georgia/practical-information/), [GNTA climate overview](https://georgia.travel/weather-and-climate/weather-and-seasons), [GNTA seasonal travel guidance](https://georgia.travel/faq).

## Decisions that change the actual trip

- **Specify the Chalaadi start.** The Svaneti DMO's drive-assisted route is about 6 km return on foot. GNTA describes a different or ambiguous distance while agreeing on the ice/stonefall hazard. The preset uses the named bridge/trailhead approach and a distant viewpoint. It does not offer glacier entry. [DMO route](https://svaneti.org/en/destinations/chalaadi-glacier) · [GNTA warning](https://georgia.travel/mestia-chalaati-glacier)
- **Split Koruldi by effort.** A full hike with roughly 1,240 m of climbing is a different product from a 4×4 outing. The walking version is graded strenuous regardless of a source's broad “easy” label. [DMO route](https://www.svaneti.org/en/destinations/koruldi%20-%20lakes)
- **Give Shkhara its own time.** The full walking route from Ushguli needs several hours. An overnight makes room for it; a firm turnaround protects the return journey, and a second night can be quoted for a slower pace. [DMO route](https://svaneti.org/en/activities/shkhara-glacier-trail) · [GNTA route](https://georgia.travel/ushguli-shkhara-glacier)
- **Do not mix Shdugra trailheads.** The preset follows TCT's Tvbishi mineral-spring start to the lower viewpoint and back. Shorter descriptions elsewhere use a different approach. If the owner changes pickup or walking start, the distance, driving and total duration must change together. [TCT Mazeri walks](https://transcaucasiantrail.org/en/hike/upper-svaneti-georgia/day-hikes-from-mazeri/)
- **Treat the Adishchala crossing as a decision, not an attraction.** The four-day trek requires current assessment, a retreat option and time to change plans. A horse or an early start does not guarantee a safe passage. The default excludes the more demanding Lagem variant. [TCT mapped route](https://transcaucasiantrail.org/en/hike/upper-svaneti-georgia/map-and-trail-notes/)
- **Keep canyon boats conditional.** Martvili boating is restricted in rain/snow; Prometheus boating can stop when water rises. Neither is a guaranteed inclusion, and a cave is not automatically an alternative during severe weather. [APA Martvili](https://apa.gov.ge/en/eco-tourism/servisebi-da-tarifebi/martvilis-kanioni) · [APA Prometheus](https://www.apa.gov.ge/en/eco-tourism/servisebi-da-tarifebi/Prometheuscave)
- **Check current lift operations.** Historical summer and winter notices establish the activity, not next season's dates or daily service. No off-piste or ski-touring promise is built into the snow-day preset. [MTA winter notice](https://mta.ski/en/presscenter/SkiSeason2026_ENG) · [MTA summer notice](https://mta.ski/en/presscenter/SummerMestia25_Eng)
- **Respect the starting city.** Kazbegi and Kakheti are eastern extensions beginning in Tbilisi. They are not advertised as Mestia day trips. Western transfers use buffered driving estimates and do not promise tight flight connections. [GNTA Mestia access](https://georgia.travel/cities-towns/mestia) · [GNTA winter travel](https://georgia.travel/svaneti-in-winter) · [GNTA Stepantsminda](https://georgia.travel/cities-towns/stepantsminda) · [GNTA Sighnaghi](https://georgia.travel/cities-towns/sighnaghi)

## Maintaining the offering

In **Admin → Services**, open a draft, review its sources and change the actual delivery details. Every guest-facing offering field is editable: titles and translations, descriptions, itinerary, duration, effort, region, start, season, meeting point, walking/driving, inclusions, exclusions, requirements, fallback, price, currency, group limit and photograph. Research URLs, research date and operator notes are private editable fields.

Publish after checking the route, supplier capability, current access, group suitability and commercial terms. Refresh source checks before each operating season, and inspect current conditions again before a departure. Useful operational sources include the [Roads Department](https://www.georoad.ge/), [National Environmental Agency](https://nea.gov.ge/En), and MTA's [Hatsvali](https://status.mta.ski/en/svaneti/hatsvali) and [Tetnuldi](https://status.mta.ski/en/svaneti/tetnuldi) status pages. Consult the actual museum, park, host and driver where needed. Historical closure notices are evidence of possible disruption, not a current closure report.

Disable hides an offering while retaining it for editing. Delete removes it from the active catalogue and new selection lists; historical enquiry and quote records remain intact. Restore brings it back as a draft. Seeding runs once and will not undo edits, republish disabled offers or recreate deleted offers on restart.

English copy is the researched starting text. Empty Georgian fields fall back to English until the owner supplies reviewed translations. No hotel allotments, driver assignments, verified qualifications, prices, weather guarantees or automatic departures have been invented.
