## Cost comparison

Prices are for ordinary, nonpremium names. Promotions and renewal prices may change.

| Registrar and extension | First year | Annual renewal | Fees and limitations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spaceship `.com` | **US$9.08** | **US$10.18** | Advertised US$8.88 / US$9.98, plus US$0.20 ICANN fee each year. Eligible domain privacy included. Applicable taxes may be additional. |
| Porkbun `.com` | US$11.08 | US$11.08 | Official pricing says ICANN and other fees are included. Free WHOIS privacy advertised. |
| CleanNet `.ge` | Advertised ₾30/year | Not separately verified | Page does not distinguish registration from renewal or confirm fee treatment. |
| Domenebi `.ge` | Advertised from ₾30 | Not verified | Starting price only; renewal breakdown not confirmed. |

**Recommendation:** a standard `.com` from Spaceship has the lowest verified renewal price among the two `.com` registrars compared. At the checked prices, the first two years total US$19.26 before applicable tax. This is a practical cost and branding choice for international visitors, not a claim that `.com` automatically improves search ranking.

A `.ge` domain is a reasonable local alternative, but confirm its renewal and taxes before choosing it. NIC.GE's older 2024 announcement describes widespread registration and renewal increases to ₾50, while current registrar pages advertise lower prices. Do not treat the old announcement or a current promotional headline as a guaranteed renewal quote.
